How it works

  1. Ask. Your buyer questions go to an AI answer engine that searches the web and cites its sources, the way Perplexity and ChatGPT search do.
  2. Count. Every cited source is recorded. Your visibility score is the share of answers that cite your site. We also check whether your site was in the search results at all, and whether the AI names you from memory.
  3. Check. Can AI crawlers read your site at all? We check robots.txt, noindex, canonicals and pages that need JavaScript.
  4. Compare. The pages that got cited are compared with yours: direct answers, numbers, headings, freshness, sources.
  5. Fix and re-check. You get a ranked fix list. Make the changes, run it again, and compare the two reports.
